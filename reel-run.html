Reel Run

Studio: Stardust Studios Engine: Unreal Engine 5 Team Size: 11-person team Timeline: Aug 2024 - Jan 2026 Role: Creative Director & Level Designer

Overview

Reel Run is a 3D arcade hack-and-slash where players star in popular movie archetypes, fighting hordes across three levels (Western, Horror, Romance). Each level is styled after its film genre and era, featuring its own playable character and destructible environments.

Play Free on Steam

Role Breakdown

Creative Director

  • Wrote design docs for our systems, mechanics, and art pipelines.
  • Directed the marketing materials and the Steam page.
  • Led the 11-person team and kept everyone working toward the same vision.
  • Set up a simple way for people to send me feedback, held team meetings where anyone could pitch ideas, and checked in at people's desks.
  • Ran playtests and iterated on the designs.
  • Designed the core mechanics and four maps.

Level Designer

  • Designed four maps: sketched in Photoshop, tested as 3D whiteboxes, then built out with environment art.
  • Did the lighting to set the mood and keep the gameplay readable.
  • Designed the first combat space, then handed it to other designers with documentation, so it became the template for later levels.
  • Spent several sprints getting the combat feel right.

Reflection

Reel Run was my most hands-on project, as both creative director and level designer. I kept asking myself whether each design fit the vision, whether other people could repeat it, and whether it made sense in the film's context.
