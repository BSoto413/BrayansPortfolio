NOW SHOWING

Reel Run

Studio: Stardust Studios Engine: Unreal Engine 5 Team: 11 people Timeline: Aug 2024 - Jan 2026 Role: Creative Director & Level Designer My levels: Western Acts 1-2, Romance Acts 1-3

Overview

Reel Run is a 3D arcade hack-and-slash where players star in popular movie archetypes, fighting hordes across three levels (Western, Horror, Romance). Each level is styled after its film genre and era, with its own playable character and destructible environments.

This page focuses on how I built my levels, from the first 2D map to the shipped game, and the design documentation I wrote along the way.

Western Act 1 · The Saloon

From Map to Finished Level

The saloon was the first level I built for Reel Run and our first real test of the game. Every level after it followed the same process.

01

Plan it in 2D

Sep 14-18, 2024

I start every level as a top-down map on a grid, with a legend for scale, cover, and where the player and enemies spawn. The saloon went through several versions in four days, opening up the floor so waves of enemies had room to come at the player.

02

Review with the team

Sep 15, 2024

I shared each version in our level design channel. Teammates marked up the map with notes on flow and combat zones, and I folded that feedback into the next revision.

03

Whitebox in Unreal

Sep 18, 2024

Next I blocked the map out in 3D with simple shapes to test scale, sightlines, and how combat actually felt in the space before any art was made.

04

Art and lighting pass

Oct-Nov 2024

Once the layout was locked, the art team built over the whitebox and I worked on lighting to set the mood and keep combat readable.

05

Ship it

Feb 2025

Once the foundation was solid, I handed the level off to other designers with clear documentation. That handoff became the standard for every level that followed.

Western Act 2

The Train

Three train cars, each with its own group of enemies: passenger, storage, and luxury. Players fight through each car and follow a critical path to the door at the end of the luxury car.

Romance Level · All three acts

Pasión en el Mar

I designed the whole Romance level, a telenovela set on a cruise ship. Each act went through two rounds of maps before I whiteboxed it.

Act 1 · The Pool Deck

Waves of enemies on the deck. Power boxes turn on spotlights that pull enemies into pose zones, where they freeze mid-pose.

Act 2 · The Dining Hall

Narrow aisles between round tables turn the room into a maze. Enemies dressed as chefs come in waves.

Act 3 · The Club Boss Fight

The boss, Ignacio, copies the player's moves while the dance floor lights up tiles in sequence. Lit tiles are electrified.

Sci-Fi Spaceship

A sci-fi level I mapped and whiteboxed in June 2025. It spans three floors with objectives like a fuel pod and computer terminals. It was cut and never made it into the game, but it shows the same map-to-whitebox process on a vertical, multi-floor space.

Reflection

Reel Run was my most hands-on project, as both creative director and level designer. I kept asking myself whether each design fit the vision, whether other people could repeat it, and whether it made sense in the film's context.
